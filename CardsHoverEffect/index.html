Rohit Sharma

The “Hitman,” explosive opener who can accelerate the innings. Brings experience and leadership.

Shubman Gill (captain)

Elegant top-order right handed batter known for consistency and stroke play. Leads India in limited overs with calm approach.

Virat Kohli

Modern great with classical technique and hunger for runs. Reliable across conditions under pressure.

Shreyas Iyer (vice-captain)

Middle order anchor with flair for counterattacking. Often steadies innings in challenging situations.

Axar Patel

Left-arm spinner who can chip in with useful lower order runs. Effective in middle overs and helpful in balance.

KL Rahul (WK)

Elegant batter and flexible keeper option. Can bat in top or middle order, adapts to role as needed.

Kuldeep Yadav

Wrist spinner with variations. Has the ability to take wickets in middle overs.

Nitish Kumar Reddy

Batting allrounder with potential to contribute with spin or seam. Young talent with all-round promise.

Dhruv Jurel (WK)

Young keeper-batter who can fill in behind the stumps. Solid technique and composure for his age.

Yashasvi Jaiswal

Aggressive left-hander who looks to dominate early. Has shown promise in domestic and limited over cricket.

Washington Sundar

Off-spinner who is useful with the bat down the order. Known for tight bowling and building pressure.

Harshit Rana

Pace prospect with ability to bowl in good rhythm. Promising young fast bowler.

Mohammed Siraj

Fast-medium bowler with accuracy and aggression. Useful with new ball and at the death.

Arshdeep Singh

Left-arm pacer with swing and control. Known for containing runs and breaking partnerships.

Prasidh Krishna

Tall fast bowler with bounce. Works well on bouncy tracks and provides depth in pace attack.
